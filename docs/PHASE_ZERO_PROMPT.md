# Phase Zero Mission

Map the commercial product market, open-source repositories, models, editing feasibility, licensing, compute economics, safety, consumer UX, and cloud development architecture before production implementation.

## Required workstreams

1. Commercial products and feature matrix.
2. Open-source video automation and editor foundations.
3. Generative image/video and controllable editing models.
4. Character consistency, segmentation, tracking, pose, motion, and identity.
5. Audio, TTS, voice cloning, captions, and alignment.
6. Research, factuality, sources, and domain routing.
7. Open-source licenses and dependency compatibility.
8. Free-use, hosting, storage, bandwidth, GPU, and rendering economics.
9. Consumer-grade conversational workflow and editing interaction.
10. Security, privacy, asset rights, voice and likeness consent.
11. Claude–Codex cloud operating model.
12. Canonical project, scene graph, edit operation, and selective-regeneration model.

## Required durable outputs

Write human-readable reports under `docs/research/` and machine-readable datasets under `data/research/`.

Every substantive row or conclusion must include:

- canonical source;
- date checked;
- source type;
- confidence;
- verification state;
- notes and uncertainty.

## Completion rule

Phase Zero ends only after:

- all workstreams exist;
- capability claims are evidence-backed;
- licenses are inspected;
- cost claims are modeled;
- MVP and deferred scope are explicit;
- an independent adversarial review is completed;
- blocker and major findings are corrected;
- a handoff package exists for Claude Opus and Codex.

Do not implement the product during Phase Zero.
