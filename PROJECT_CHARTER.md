# Project Charter

## Product

Build an open-source conversational video-production studio that lets non-technical users move from any lawful YouTube idea to a researched, scripted, narrated, visually composed, reviewed, revised, and release-ready video package.

## Product promise

Describe the video. Review the direction. Give feedback naturally. Receive a polished, editable, release-ready YouTube package.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Consumer-grade usability.
- Provider-independent architecture.
- Structured editable project and scene graph.
- Selective regeneration rather than full regeneration by default.
- Version history and rollback.
- Source and asset-rights traceability.
- Explicit consent for likeness and voice use.
- Human approval before publication.
- Free and open-source core.
- Honest separation of free software from paid compute.
- Cloud-native development that does not depend on the owner’s laptop.
- Claude plans, researches, specifies, and reviews.
- Codex implements approved task contracts and repairs verified findings.
- GitHub is the durable source of truth.

## Phase Zero

No production implementation begins until the market, repository, model, license, cost, safety, UX, and technical-feasibility audit is complete and adversarially reviewed.
