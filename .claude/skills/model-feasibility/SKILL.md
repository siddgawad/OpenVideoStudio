Evaluate these capabilities, models, or tasks:

$ARGUMENTS

For every capability state:

- demonstrated support;
- reliability and preservation risk;
- required references, masks, controls and inputs;
- compute and latency;
- license and commercial use;
- safety and consent concerns;
- MVP, beta, future or reject classification;
- fallback path.

Do not equate a compelling demo with reliable product capability.
