---
name: Source Register Discipline
description: Apply whenever research facts, prices, licenses, repository status, model capabilities, or current technical claims are recorded.
user-invocable: false
---

For each substantive finding, record:

- stable identifier;
- canonical source URL;
- source organization;
- source type;
- title;
- publication or release date;
- retrieval date;
- quoted or paraphrased evidence;
- confidence;
- verification state;
- whether the fact is time-sensitive;
- uncertainty;
- affected conclusions.

Prefer primary sources. Distinguish fact, inference, recommendation, and unresolved claim.

Do not add a conclusion to final reports unless its evidence exists in the source register.
